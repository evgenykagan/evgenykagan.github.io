Conversational AI is increasingly built into the search interfaces of online platforms. In this paper we run a pre-registered online experiment in which participants search a catalog of several thousand live events using one of three interfaces: an attribute-based filter interface, a GenAI chat interface, or a combination of the two. We find that the chat-only interface performs worst. Two mechanisms drive the result: users cannot see what the catalog contains, so they search for events that are not there, and they shift effort towards prompting and away from evaluating the output."
