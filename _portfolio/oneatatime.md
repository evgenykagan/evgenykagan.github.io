<b>Co-authored with Brett Hathaway and John Jones.</b> <br> Emergency departments often use a shared-queue setup in which physicians self-assign cases from a pool of triaged patients. Using data from five emergency departments and experiments with healthcare workers and ED physicians, we show that batching, i.e., self-assigning multiple patients at once, is common, is associated with longer stays for batched patients, and persists even when it reduces physicians' own payoffs, a behavior we term the personal productivity bias."
