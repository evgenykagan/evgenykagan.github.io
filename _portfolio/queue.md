How a queue of jobs is arranged and presented to workers is an important design problem in service operations. In this paper we use online experiments to examine how job ordering, worker autonomy and queue visibility affect the speed and quality of work. We find that workers who choose their own job order perform worse than workers with an imposed order, and that seeing new jobs arrive produces a short-term productivity burst."
